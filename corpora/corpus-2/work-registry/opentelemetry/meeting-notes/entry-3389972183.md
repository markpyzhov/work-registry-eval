### 1) Incident review: go.opentelemetry.io outage, ownership, and platform

- Summary:
  - Recent outage on go.opentelemetry.io due to a CAA DNS entry change that blocked certificate issuance for Google-managed certs.
  - Current hosting runs a Go app on Google App Engine with Google-managed certificates; proposal to migrate the Go Vanity URLs to a static Hugo site on Netlify (aligning with how opentelemetry.io is operated).
  - Core concern raised: the outage root cause was lack of clear ownership, permissions, and change notification, not the hosting platform itself. Moving platforms without fixing ownership/ops may reduce control further.

- Discussion highlights:
  - Platform standardization (Hugo/Netlify) could reduce “snowflake” risk, but does not resolve core issues of:
    - Clear ownership and responsibilities
    - Access/permissions for maintainers (DNS, hosting, rollout)
    - Notifications and checks for changes impacting serving (e.g., DNS/CAA updates)
    - Incident playbooks and operational processes
  - Agreement that the SIG needs either definitive ownership and access or a well-defined delegation with checks-and-balances to whoever operates the domain and hosting.
  - Multiple projects rely on go.opentelemetry.io vanity URLs (Go SDK, Collector, build tools, Go instrumentation, eBPF instrumentation, proto, etc.), so any changes require broad communication.

- Decisions:
  - Proceed to define and confirm ownership, access, and operational processes first; explore migration in parallel only with the current or future owners’ buy-in.
  - Ensure maintainers responsible for packages served under go.opentelemetry.io have appropriate access or guaranteed oversight channels.

- Action items:
  - Damien: Create a community issue to drive a cross-team ownership and operations proposal; invite GC (Governing Committee), comms/docs team, Collector maintainers, Go instrumentation, eBPF instrumentation, eBPF profiler, and this SIG.
  - Damien: Propose strawman ownership models (Go SIG owning vs. comms/docs owning), required access (“keys”), and operational processes (DNS change notifications, rollout, playbooks).
  - Tyler: Outline notification/subscription approach for DNS/CAA changes affecting serving; suggest checks-and-balances process.
  - Group: Align on clear owner, permissions, and ops plan before any platform migration decision.

### 2) PR: Add internal observability to OTLP Trace gRPC package; instrumentation scope naming

- Summary:

  - Debate on scope name: use the instrumentation library/package path (current proposal) vs. the instrumented package/component name.

- Discussion highlights:

### 4) PR: Counter performance improvements; benchmarks and exemplar behavior

- Summary:
  - Significant optimization work on synchronous metric paths:
    - Use sync.Map for attribute-set storage to improve read-heavy performance.
    - Atomic sum values; “hot-cold” waitgroup technique to make measurements lockless and enable safe resets for delta aggregations (inspired by Prometheus histogram patterns).
  - Known implications:
    - Attribute-set limit becomes a soft limit due to race in attribute-set creation (map size tracking vs. map updates).
    - For cumulative aggregations, enforcing attribute limits deterministically is harder since state persists; care needed to avoid inconsistent drops.
    - Exemplar behavior: an Add may be included in the metric points but its exemplar may not be recorded in the same batch, which is acceptable and consistent with Prometheus and exemplar sampling design.

- Discussion highlights:
  - Potential follow-up: for exemplar reservoirs, a “merge” API could enable hot-cold reservoir consolidation for stricter atomicity at a higher level.
  - Future performance work: histogram improvements and sharding (e.g., per-CPU) to reduce contention further.
  - Benchmarks indicate substantial gains: ~10x for histograms with no contention; ~3x for fixed-size with shared contention.

- Decisions:
  - Accept current approach; exemplar variability is acceptable under spec/practice.
  - Prioritize reviews for the benchmark PR and counter performance PR; histograms/fixed-size follow after.

- Action items:
