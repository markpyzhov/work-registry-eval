### Stabilization of otelgrpc
- Community request to stabilize otelgrpc noted by Tyler.
- Blockers:
  - Stabilization of RPC semantic conventions (semconv) must come first.
  - Audit of the current otelgrpc API to remove cruft and confirm stability.
- Damien clarified that stabilization does not imply restoring the old interceptor—it should remain removed.
- David is willing to drive module stability once semconv stabilizes; however, gRPC engagement has been sporadic and driven by their internal roadmap.
- Strategy discussion:
  - OTel could continue owning and maintaining a gRPC instrumentation package, with the option to deprecate later if gRPC ships native instrumentation aligned with semconv.
  - Seek more engagement from gRPC; attempt in-person conversations at KubeCon if possible. David will sync with Josh on outreach.
- Conclusion: Not likely to stabilize this year; set a goal to finalize a strategy next year once semconv progress is clearer.

### SIG Security: release signing
- Robert proposed enabling GitHub “signature releases” in addition to existing tag signing and GPG signatures of source archives.
- Key points:
  - GitHub’s source tarball/zip is generated at download time; signing artifacts strengthens trust and mitigates changes over time.
  - Double-signing is acceptable and beneficial for OSS Scorecard/compliance.
  - Constraint: Artifacts must be uploaded at release time; cannot regenerate later.
- Decision: Proceed with enabling signature releases; update release documentation and ensure process discipline.

References:
- [https://github.com/open-telemetry/sig-security/issues/164](https://github.com/open-telemetry/sig-security/issues/164)
