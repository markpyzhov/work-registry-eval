# License compliance looked cleared after a PDF-parser swap, then changed again. As of **2026-09-22**, what is the current license-gate status, what discovery reopened it, and what replacement criteria are in force?

<!-- RESPONSE_START -->

As of 2026-09-22, T030/CIPH-40 remains **reopened and active** under a **P0 hard Legal gate**; it is not cleared. An AGPL dependency in the shipped bundle creates whole-bundle copyleft exposure for the distribution model, so an engineering library search alone cannot close it. [WS-04-policy](/Users/markpyzhov/VSCodeProjects/corpuses-arms/c1/work-states/WS-04-policy.md), [Aug. 12 Legal comment](/Users/markpyzhov/VSCodeProjects/corpuses-arms/c1/work-states/comments/2026-08.md), [Sep. 22 brief](/Users/markpyzhov/VSCodeProjects/corpuses-arms/c1/briefs/2026-09-22-daily-brief.md)

It reopened when follow-up review found that the replacement PDF parser—initially treated as completing the swap—was itself **AGPL-licensed**. The initial audit had identified AGPLv3 Ghostscript in the rasterizer; the replacement removed that specific dependency but recreated the same prohibited license category downstream. [AGPL reopen thread](/Users/markpyzhov/VSCodeProjects/corpuses-arms/c1/med-synth/communication/sources/slack/2026-08-12-license-agpl-reopen.md), [license audit report](/Users/markpyzhov/VSCodeProjects/corpuses-arms/c1/med-synth/documentation/sources/2026-08-21-License-Compliance-Audit-Report.md)

The required replacement must be **Apache 2.0 or MIT licensed**, meet the PDF module’s required parsing/rasterization capability, pass a clean scan, be adopted into the shipped set, and leave the full dependency tree free of restrictive copyleft such as AGPL. No suitable replacement had been confirmed.
