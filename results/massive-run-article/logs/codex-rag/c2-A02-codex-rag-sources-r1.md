# On what date will otelgrpc module stabilization be complete?

<!-- RESPONSE_START -->

No completion date was set. The Go SIG said otelgrpc was **not likely to stabilize in 2025** and aimed to finalize a strategy in **2026**, after RPC semantic conventions progressed. [SRC-NOTE-06](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-go/issues/6648#issuecomment-3479655348)
