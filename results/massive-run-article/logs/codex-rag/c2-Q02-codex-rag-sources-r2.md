# What did the Go SIG decide to do with Nico’s large runtime-tracer / profiling public-API proposal?

<!-- RESPONSE_START -->

The Go SIG decided **not to merge the large public-API change yet**. They would keep Nico’s PR as a draft/proof of concept, first open an issue documenting the profiling proposal and its trade-offs, and proceed with a small incremental change—such as letting users disable runtime-tracer integration. [SRC-NOTE-08](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-go/issues/6648#issuecomment-3974113940)
